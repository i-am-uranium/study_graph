## Design Context

### Users

StudyGraph is for students and teachers who turn learning materials into usable study and classroom outputs. The current product is local-first and single-user, but the long-term direction is a smart learning studio with student, teacher, and administrator roles, collaboration, progress tracking, assignments, analytics, integrations, offline access, and secure sync.

### Brand Personality

StudyGraph should feel like a hybrid learning studio: calm, capable, and trustworthy. It should support focused self-study and teacher workflows without feeling like a generic admin dashboard or a playful toy.

### Aesthetic Direction

Use a Clean Study OS direction: modern workspace layout, restrained color, clear hierarchy, high readability, and strong light/dark theme parity. Keep innovation practical and learning-centered, especially around source trust, citations, and source-to-output flow.

### Design Principles

- Make source material the foundation of the experience: uploads, readiness, citations, and generated outputs should feel connected.
- Treat themes as first-class: light and dark modes need equivalent contrast, surfaces, and state clarity.
- Design mobile as a real workflow surface with bottom navigation, large touch targets, and no hidden core actions.
- Make AI output inspectable: citations, confidence notes, and draft quality should be visible rather than buried.
- Keep the current implementation shippable while leaving obvious room for future roles, assignments, collaboration, analytics, sync, and offline workflows.
